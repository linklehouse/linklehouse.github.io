Supplier sells out,
the listing pauses

Supplier stock and status are checked every 30 minutes. Sold-out or discontinued items are paused on every marketplace, and restored when they come back.

  • Prevents cancellations and penalties from phantom stock
  • Pauses only the sold-out option
  • Paced to respect supplier daily API limits

WHY PRODUCE

Fresh produce isn't
like other products

General e-commerce tools don't fit these well, so we built for them from day one.

3kg · 5kg · 10kgWeight, size & count options

The same apple comes in many weights, counts and sizes. Options are reshaped to fit each marketplace.

Gift · Home · UglyQuality grades

Grades are read from supplier descriptions, and at the same price the better grade is chosen.

SeasonalSell-outs & price swings

Supply stops with the season and prices move. Checks every 30 minutes and daily keep listings current.

Origin · harvest yearProduct disclosures

Each marketplace's required notice fields are filled with produce-specific defaults.

Holiday gift setsSets by what's inside

A beef set and a dried-persimmon set are told apart by the items they contain.

Rice · Kimchi · SeafoodRules per category

Rice by region and brand, kimchi by type, seafood by cut and frozen state — so duplicates are caught.

PRICING RULE

No sale
at a loss

Cost includes the supplier's shipping fee. Selling prices are set on real margin, after marketplace fees and ad spend.

  • Cost = supplier price + supplier shipping (one box)
  • Real margin after per-market fees and per-product ad spend
  • Raise when cost rises; keep the price when it falls
