How it works

The repetitive steps a person used to do by hand, done in order.

List products

Pick supplier products and list them following each marketplace's rules.

Collect orders

New orders from every marketplace are gathered in one place every 30 minutes.

Order from suppliers

Each order is matched to its supplier product and purchased right away.

Ship & track

When the supplier ships, tracking numbers are posted back to each marketplace.

Features

Less time jumping between marketplace admin pages all day.

Order intake & auto purchasing

Collects new orders from five marketplaces, confirms them, and places them with the linked supplier automatically.

Tracking number sync

Picks up supplier tracking numbers and marks orders as shipped on every marketplace. Only failures are flagged.

Supplier cost watch

When supplier prices or shipping fees go up, selling prices are raised to match so no order sells at a loss.

Out-of-stock watch

When a supplier sells out or ends a product, it is taken off sale on the marketplaces too.

AI-assisted listing

AI drafts product names, search tags and categories, builds the product detail page, and lists it per marketplace.

Inquiries & claims

Sorts customer questions by type and drafts replies. Cancellations and returns are gathered in one view.
